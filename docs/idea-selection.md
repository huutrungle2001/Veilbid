## 4. Selected concept

**VeilBid — Confidential Procurement for Safe Treasuries**

One-sentence value:

> A Safe treasury can award and pay the lowest valid vendor bid without
> publishing vendor prices, while preserving public rules and selective audit.

## 5. Why it is distinct

- Nox selects both encrypted best price and associated encrypted bid ID.
- Public decryption reveals the winner, not the price.
- ERC-7984 performs confidential payment and remainder refund.
- Safe remains treasury authority.
- Permissionless finalizers provide liveness without private access.
- Buyer/vendor/auditor roles create a product, not only a contract demo.
